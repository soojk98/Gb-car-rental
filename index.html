Questions.

What documents do I need to prepare?

Please prepare a copy of your NRIC, your Competent Driving Licence (CDL), and your e-hailing PSV licence. If you do not have a PSV yet, tell us and we will guide you through it.

Is there a deposit required?

Yes, a standard refundable deposit is required before you collect the vehicle. This protects both parties. Contact us for the exact deposit on your chosen car.

Who pays for maintenance and servicing?

We do. Routine servicing, oil changes and normal wear-and-tear maintenance are fully covered by GB Car Rental. Just bring the car to our panel workshop when it is due.
