Questions

Straight answers.

What do I need to pay before I can drive?

Deposit amounts — owner to confirm Your deposit plus the first week’s rental. Nothing else — no admin fee, no processing charge.

What documents do I need?

Your NRIC and your Competent Driving Licence (CDL). If you do not have a PSV licence yet, tell us — we arrange it and we pay for it.

What is the minimum rental period?

Owner to confirm

Who pays for servicing and repairs?

We do. Scheduled servicing, tyres, brake pads, battery, wipers and bulbs are all covered. Bring the car to our panel workshop when it is due. Damage caused by driver negligence is yours.

What happens if I have an accident?

Owner to confirm the procedure What we can say now: the car carries comprehensive insurance with zero excess, so an insured claim costs you nothing.

Who pays for fuel and traffic summonses?

Fuel is yours. Summonses issued while the car is with you are yours to settle.
